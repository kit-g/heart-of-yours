# Licence (#482)

The app is GPL-3.0 because the one thing worth guarding is a closed copy of it in a store, and
copyleft lets anyone learn from it, reuse it or ship their own build as long as theirs stays open
too; that matches the server's AGPL-3.0 (heart-api's `docs/2026-10-24.licence.md`), whose network
clause would add nothing to a client. The GPL alone conflicts with the App Store's terms for
anyone but the copyright holder, and the app links Firebase and Google Play services, so `NOTICE`
adds two section 7 permissions, store distribution and platform SDKs. They travel with every
contribution under inbound = outbound, so contributed code can ship through the stores without a
CLA. The packages in `shared/` take the app's licence: they exist for this app and are not
published, unlike `heart_models` and `heart_design`, which come from heart-api under Apache-2.0.
The name and icon are trademarks no licence grants, the store copy and README screenshots stay
all rights reserved, and `NOTICE` lists the fonts, logos and artwork that keep their own terms.
