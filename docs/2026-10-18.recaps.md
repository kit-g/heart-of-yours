# Recaps: the month, the year, and the card that shares them

Three tickets with one engine underneath: the monthly report (#154), the year in review
(#155) and share cards (#166). All three are opt-in as one feature, `Feature.recaps`. The year
in review has to be ready for December 2026. This note covers what the engine computes, where
the recaps should surface, and what a card may show.

## The engine

`recapOf` in `heart_state` (`src/recaps.dart`) is a pure fold over a `RecapPeriod`, which is
a calendar month or a calendar year in the user's zone. A workout belongs to the period it
started in, as every other count in the app has it. `Stats.getRecap(period, firstWeekday:,
weightUnit:)` runs the two local reads (`getRecapWorkouts` and `getRecapSets` in `heart_db`)
and folds them. Nothing is stored. Off costs nothing, and on finds the whole history ready,
because it is computed from what the user has already logged (`docs/opt-in.md`, *Derived
data is optional*).

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| `workouts`, `trained`, `trainingDays` | finished workouts started in the period; their time, start to end less pauses; distinct calendar days |
| `sets` | completed working sets |
| `volume` | Σ weight × reps over lifted sets (barbell, dumbbell, machine, weighted body weight), converted to the user's unit |
| `muscles` | sets, sessions and volume per muscle group, most sets first, by the muscle map's rule (`groupSharesOf`) |
| `patterns` | the same per movement pattern, by the pattern lens's rule (`patternsOf`, `countByPeriod`) |
| `exercises` | per exercise, most-logged first |
| `records` | records set in the period that still stand at its end, as the entries from the shared fold (`PersonalRecords.toPersonalRecords`); `isFirst` marks an exercise's first time |
| `lifts` | each lifted exercise's heaviest set and best e1RM within the period alone |
| `weeks`, `weeksTrained` | workouts per week (the user's first day, #339), zeros included; weeks with any training |
| `months` | workouts per calendar month: the year's twelve bars |

Rules it keeps:

- **One definition of each thing.** Muscle shares, patterns and records come from the code the
  map, the lens and the records screen already use, so a recap never disagrees with them.
  `groupSharesOf` moved into `heart_state` for this, and the app's `muscleSharesOf` now maps
  its result onto the atlas.
- **Warm-ups never count**, the same as for records and the muscle map. Unfinished workouts
  never count either.
- **Records need history.** The set read pulls every working set of each exercise trained in
  the period, from its first time up to the period's end, so a record can say what it beat. A
  record set mid-month and beaten later the same month is not listed: only records still
  standing at the end are.
- **No streaks.** Consistency is counted in weeks trained out of weeks that have begun. Nothing
  can be broken, so nothing has to be kept up.
- **Never a health value.** The workout read selects id, start, end and pauses, and nothing a
  health source wrote (`docs/2026-09-05.health-data.md`). No field on `Recap` may ever carry
  one, because a share card renders straight from it.

## Where it surfaces (proposal)

Annoy as little as possible (`docs/style.md`, *UX*). A recap is something you find, not
something that finds you.

- **Monthly card.** A quiet card on Profile for the first days of a month (proposed: the 1st
  to the 7th): last month in a line (workouts, time, one record if any). Tapping it opens the
  monthly report. It does not show for a month with nothing in it, and it goes away on its own.
  There is no dismiss button to manage and no badge.
- **An entry under the profile.** A "Recaps" row that lists past months and years for anyone
  who wants to look back. It is always there while the feature is on.
- **Year in review.** From mid-December (proposed: the 15th) to the end of January, the
  Profile card shows the year instead of the month. The review is a short sequence of pages:
  totals, months, most-trained muscles and patterns, most-logged exercises, best lifts,
  records, and weeks trained.
- **No notifications**, for any of them.
- **The ask.** Rule 1 of the opt-in contract: the first time a card would appear (the first
  week of a month after any workouts were logged), it appears as the offer instead. Off, the
  card, the row and the year are never built. Until that UI lands, the switch stays out of
  Settings › Features, because a switch with nothing behind it would be a dead control.

## What a share card may show

A share card is an image the user chooses to send, rendered on the device. It may show:

- the period, workouts, time trained, training days, weeks trained
- sets and volume (in the user's unit), most-trained muscles and patterns
- most-logged exercises, best lifts, and records with what they beat
- the Heart mark and heart-of.me (#166 leaves the link open)

It never shows a health value. That rules out heart rate, calories, HRV, sleep, steps and body
weight, and anything else read from HealthKit or Health Connect. It also never shows the
user's name or photo unless they add it on the card themselves (#166). Sharing is not an
analytics event that carries any value from the card.

## Open

- The exact days the monthly card and the year in review show, and whether the review opens
  itself once on the first launch after the 15th, which this note proposes it should not.
- How many records the monthly card shows when a month has many, and whether first-time
  records count there.
- Best lifts are sorted by e1RM across exercises, which favours big compound lifts. The year
  in review may prefer the most-logged lifted exercises instead. The engine keeps every lift
  and the screen decides.
