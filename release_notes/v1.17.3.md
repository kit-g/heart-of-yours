# 1.17.3

Android only. One fix.

## Exercise names in History and templates were in English on a phone set to another language

The exercise library is downloaded in your language, but workouts, templates and programs come from the server, which named their exercises in English. History swapped the phone's own copies for the server's on every launch, so History and templates read in English while the library read in your language. The app now names those exercises from its own library, so they match the library everywhere. A renamed exercise of your own shows its new name in History straight away, for the same reason.

When the library can't name one of your exercises, the app reports it (how many, and the phone's language) so it can be found and fixed.
