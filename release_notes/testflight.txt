Health data on your profile
Heart can now read resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, steps,
active energy and body mass from Apple Health and chart them on your profile.
It stays on this device — nothing is uploaded — and you can delete Heart's copy
whenever you like. When you grant access, allow past data too, or the charts
only reach back thirty days.

Charts you can scroll
Exercise and health charts now go back through your whole history instead of a
few weeks. Switch between 1M, 3M, 1Y and All, drag to move through time, and tap
any health card for the full chart with axes.

VoiceOver
Buttons, charts, timers and inputs across the app now announce themselves.

Smaller things
- Deleting a workout from your history asks first.
- The rest timer follows the exercise you're actually resting on.
- The Previous column no longer shows an empty pill.
- Opening a Heart link from outside the app lands on the right page, even from
  a cold start.

What to test
- Grant health access, revoke it in the Health app, then grant it again. The
  profile should catch up on its own when you come back, with no restart.
- Scroll a chart back past the point where your data runs out.
- Turn VoiceOver on and walk through a workout, start to finish.
