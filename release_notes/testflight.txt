Bring your Strong history

Lifted with Strong before? Import your whole history: Settings → Your data →
Import workout history. Pick the CSV file from Strong's export email and Heart
brings over your workouts, sets and exercises. Before anything is written you
see what the file holds — and exercises that don't match Heart's library are
yours to approve one by one, so junk names stay behind. Importing the same
file twice is safe: anything already here is skipped, never duplicated.

Smaller things
- Settings are organized into sections — appearance, units, health, your data,
  account and app each have their own place, and the section headers announce
  themselves to VoiceOver.

What to test
- Settings → Your data → Import workout history, pick your Strong CSV. Uncheck
  one suggested exercise before importing, then check History lands everything
  in the right months and the report matches what you chose.
- Import the same file again — it should say nothing is new and add nothing.
- Cancel out of the exercise-approval step — nothing should be imported.
