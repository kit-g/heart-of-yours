Warm-ups, drop sets and failure sets
Tap a set's number to mark it. Warm-ups stay out of your records, charts and
muscle map, and Previous lines them up with last time's warm-ups. Your Apple
Watch shows them too.

RPE, if you want it
Rate how hard each set felt, 6 to 10, from the same menu on its number. It's
off until you turn it on in Settings › Features. RPE from a Strong import is
kept either way.

A note on the whole workout
Add one from the workout's ⋯ menu. It shows on the workout's card in History.

Muscles worked, per workout
With the muscle map on, every finished workout shows which muscles it worked,
right after you finish and in History.

Fill a column at once
Tap kg or Reps above your sets to fill every set not yet done from the top
one. Tap ✓ to tick them all.

Also
- Starting a workout while another is running now asks first.
- Settings › Features has a page of its own, and What's new takes you
  straight to a feature's switch.

What to test
- Mark sets W / D / F from their number; tap the same type again to make it
  plain. Only plain sets are numbered (W, 1, 2, F).
- Finish a workout with a warm-up heavier than your best: no record, and it
  stays out of charts and the muscle map.
- Next session of that exercise: Previous shows last time's warm-up on the
  warm-up row.
- Apple Watch: marked sets show W / D / F, numbered like the phone.
- Turn RPE on in Settings › Features. Rate a set from its number: its cell
  gets a small @8. Clear it with × or by tapping the rating again. Turn RPE
  off: ratings hide, and come back when you turn it on.
- Edit a workout imported from Strong that has warm-ups or RPE, save,
  reopen: nothing lost.
- Add, edit and remove a workout note, in a running workout and from History.
- Tap kg and Reps above the sets: unticked sets fill. ✓ ticks all; tap again
  to untick.
- With a workout running, start another: Heart asks before replacing it.
- Tap a Live Activity while the workout is already on screen: nothing jumps.
- What's new: the muscle map note's button opens Features at its switch.
