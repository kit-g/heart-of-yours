Your month in numbers, your training for an AI, and records as they happen.

- Recaps: early each month, last month on your profile: workouts, time,
  sets, volume, the muscles and patterns you trained, the records you set.
- Copy for AI: a workout, a month or one exercise's history as text any
  assistant reads well. Nothing from Apple Health goes with it.
- Live records: a quiet note the moment a ticked set beats your best.
- Second look: Heart points out entries that look like a slip, like a
  workout left running for hours, and you keep each one or leave it out
  of your records and charts.
- Log a past workout: say when it was, then what you did, and it goes
  straight to History.
- Create an exercise from the picker while you build a template or a
  workout, and tag the muscles it works on the body map.
- Apple Watch: time a hold from the wrist.
- Sets a week and the example templates now have switches in Settings.
  Both stay on unless you turn them off.

What to test
- Recaps: turn it on, check the profile card and the Recaps list, open
  last month's report, share it. A month with no workouts.
- Copy for AI: from a History workout's menu, then a month, then an
  exercise's history. Copy, Share, switching tabs. Paste it somewhere.
- Live records: the offer under the first set that would be a record,
  then a record on a weight, on reps, on a warm-up (it should not count).
- Second look (signed in): a workout left running, an odd weight. Keep
  one, leave one out, and check the charts and records follow.
- Past workouts: turn on in Settings > Features, log one from History,
  move the start and watch the end follow, finish with an untouched set.
- Create an exercise from the picker with a filter on, with a name
  already in the library, in Cyrillic.
- A template with only spaces as its name should not save.
- Apple Watch: start a hold's stopwatch from the watch, stop it, see
  the time on the phone. Rest screen: -10s, +10s and Skip on one row.
- Finish a workout on iPad: the sheet holds still while the dialog is up.
- Turn Sets a week and Example templates off and on: the profile and
  Workout tab reflow, and Siri shortcuts follow.
