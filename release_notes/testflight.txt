Your workout on the lock screen
Elapsed time, the next set and your rest countdown, on the lock screen and in
the Dynamic Island. Off until you turn it on in Settings.

Rest timers in one place
Settings now lists every rest timer you have set, with a way to clear one —
and an undo if you didn't mean it.

What's new
Settings now has a What's new page: what each update brought, and which
version you are on.

Fixed
Tapping a workout notification opens the workout you left running, instead of
wherever you were last.

What to test
- Turn the lock screen workout on, start a workout, lock the phone. Time, next
  set and rest should keep up, and tapping it should open the workout.
- Leave it off and confirm nothing appears.
- Settings > Rest timers: set timers on a few exercises mid-workout, clear one,
  undo it.
- Sign in with email and with Apple. The sign-in libraries moved in this build,
  so this is the likeliest thing to break.
- Cold start, then start a workout four ways: blank, from a template, from an
  example, and by repeating a past one. All four paths changed.
- Export your data, and import a Strong CSV. Both were touched.
- Anything that loads over the network — exercises, history, images — should
  look normal. The app's networking was rewrapped underneath.
