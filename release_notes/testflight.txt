Heart on your Home Screen
Three new widgets in the widget gallery:
- Training: your volume, workouts, time, sets or reps over the last week
  or months, or a reading from Apple Health instead. It fits the Lock
  Screen too.
- Start: one tap starts a workout or one of your templates.
- Goals: how far along your goals are.

Start from the icon
With Shortcuts on, touch and hold Heart's icon to start a blank workout
or one of your templates.

Move an exercise's history
Logged under the wrong exercise, or made your own before finding it in
the library? Move history to... on the exercise's menu puts everything
onto another exercise. Needs an account.

Bring your Heart history back
Import now reads Heart's own exports. The JSON file brings back
everything: workouts, templates and folders, custom exercises and goals.
The CSV brings workouts and their sets.

Plus fixes, including a weight of 0 that now sticks, on the watch too.

What to test
- Add each widget (Home Screen, small and medium, and the Lock Screen).
  Edit the Training widget: switch between volume, workouts, time, sets
  and reps, then try an Apple Health reading. Empty or stale numbers
  are the failure.
- Tap the Start widget from a cold start and with the app in the
  background: the workout or template it names should open, started.
- Touch and hold the app icon: start a blank workout and a template.
  With Shortcuts off in Settings > Features, Heart's items are gone.
- Move an exercise's history: the preview counts match, the move lands,
  and the page becomes the target's. Not offered during a workout that
  holds the exercise.
- Export as JSON, erase or use another phone, import it: workouts,
  templates, folders, custom exercises and goals all come back.
- During a workout, set a weight to 0 (typed and from Previous). The
  watch and the lock screen show 0, and Done on the watch keeps it.
- Sign in with Apple: one sheet, straight in.
