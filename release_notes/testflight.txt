Bug fixes and performance improvements.

What to test
- Your own exercise, on a slow connection: tap Save, then try to close
  the dialog (back, the close button, a tap outside) before it finishes.
  It stays open until the save is done, closes itself, and the screen
  behind it still works.
- A workout with a very long exercise name (your own, or in Russian):
  the name wraps or is cut short, and the timer and options buttons
  stay on screen and work.
- History: an exercise with sets you planned but never ticked shows no
  best set, and total volume counts ticked sets only.
- A weekly goal on a top set or estimated 1RM: its line now shows on
  the exercise's charts and on its Profile chart.
- iPad, both orientations: Exercises and History tabs, select
  something, clear it, switch tabs.
