Heart now speaks Spanish, French and Russian

Set your device to Spanish, French or Russian and everything follows — every
screen, and the exercise library itself: names, technique instructions,
filters, muscle targets. Copy that was machine-translated and not yet reviewed
by a human carries a small "Machine-translated" mark, so you always know what
you're reading. Switch languages any time; the library refreshes on the spot.
Canadian English keeps its date conventions.

Pick your look

Settings → Theme offers four presets — Forge, Ink, Utility and Ember — each
with its own typography and hand-tuned palettes for both light and dark. The
whole app was repainted around them: cards, dialogs, buttons, charts, down to
the check that marks a finished set.

Records, front and centre

The exercise Records tab is rebuilt: best set volume, most reps, best pace, a
rep-max table, and lifetime totals — sessions, first performed, total time and
distance. Finish a workout that sets a personal record and the summary screen
celebrates it.

Smaller things

- Workout cards in History lead with the numbers that matter and list the full
  exercise roster.
- Add an exercise to your current workout right from its page.
- Put any chart from an exercise's Charts tab onto your profile dashboard with
  one tap — and take it back off.
- An exercise's History tab updates the moment you edit a workout, and its
  cards open the editor directly.

Fixes: dialog buttons, card outlines and the muscle atlas now sit properly on
every theme; settings spacing.

What to test

- Switch your device language to Spanish, French or Russian; browse the
  exercise library and open an exercise's Description — look for the
  "Machine-translated" mark.
- Settings → Theme: try all four presets, in light and dark.
- Open a favourite exercise → Records, then finish a workout with a personal
  best and check the completion screen.
- Log a workout in another language, then switch back — history, charts and
  records should carry over seamlessly.
