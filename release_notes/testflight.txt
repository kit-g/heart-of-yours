Your workouts in Apple Health

Finished workouts now save to Apple Health, so your rings credit your lifting.
Heart writes how long you trained and what kind of session it was — a swim logs
as a swim, a lift as strength training — and nothing else.

It never estimates calories. Without a watch there is no measurement, only a
guess, and a made-up number sitting next to your watch's own reading is worse
than no number at all.

If you have already connected Apple Health, Heart asks once, right after you
finish a workout. There's a "Save workouts to Health" row in Heart's settings
to turn it on or off later.

Smaller things
- The weekly chart on your profile no longer runs its dates together.

What to test
- Finish a workout and allow the prompt, then look in Apple Health under
  Browse › Activity › Workouts for the session and its length.
- Finish a cardio session — a swim, run or cycle — and check it is labelled as
  that rather than as strength training.
- Decline the prompt, then turn it back on from Heart's settings.
