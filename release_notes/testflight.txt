Programs: train to a plan, not just a log.

Pick one of 19 ready-made programs, each with the research behind it, or
turn a folder of your templates into one. Heart schedules the sessions,
fills in the targets and adds weight when you earn it. Starting weights
come from your own history, and you can set any lift's by hand.

- Not today: set a session, or any day, aside with one tap. Say why if you like.
- Things to go easy on: a knee, your spine, overhead pressing. Heart marks
  what is harder on them and suggests kinder swaps.
- Your program's sessions in your calendar.
- Supersets and circuits.
- Rep ranges and targets in templates.
- Sets a week, by muscle or movement pattern, and how push and pull,
  hinge and squat balance.
- Goals on a movement pattern or a muscle.
- Records show on the set that set them, in History too.
- Choose the day your week starts.
- Previous can show the last time you ran that template.

What to test
- Start a ready-made program: the days it asks for, and the starting
  weights it takes from your history.
- A program session: targets on every set, the note at the top, finishing
  it, and the Done page's training max line with Undo.
- Not today on a program day, then Undo.
- Update over 1.14 with a workout left open: it should resume and finish.
- Calendar: the permission prompt, and the events in Calendar.
- Back from a program reached from History, a chart or Profile returns
  where you were.
- iPad: the program page and the library in both orientations.
