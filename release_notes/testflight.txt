Heart now works without an account.

• Start straight away. No sign-up: your workouts, templates, goals and any Apple Health data you allow live on this phone and nowhere else.
• A short intro on first launch explains the trade. Skip it any time.
• Log in whenever you like, and everything you logged before you did is backed up to your account. Your profile shows it going up.
• Your full workout history now downloads in the background, so charts, goals and records count everything you have done — not just the recent sessions your phone had already fetched.
• Export my data, in Settings: your whole log as a JSON or CSV file through the share sheet. Health data is never included.
• Erase my data, in Settings: wipe everything on this phone when you are not signed in.

Fixed: workouts could lose their exercises after syncing; an abandoned session could come back as a workout in progress; dialogs now follow a light/dark switch while open; charts stay readable on very busy weeks.

What to test

• Fresh install: walk the intro, log a workout, then open History and your profile — all without signing in.
• Log in from that same session and watch the backup line on your profile run to the end.
• On an account with a long history, open the profile after launch and watch the weekly chart fill in.
• Settings › Export my data: try both JSON and CSV, and save one to Files.
• Settings › Erase my data while signed out, and confirm the app starts over.
