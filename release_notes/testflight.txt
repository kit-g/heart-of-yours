Heart on Apple Watch
Log your sets from the wrist: the set you're on with last time's numbers,
the rest countdown, your heart rate and calories. Swipe for the whole
workout to fix a set you already logged. Phone in your locker? The watch
keeps logging, and your iPhone catches up when it's back. Open Heart on
your watch to turn it on.

Muscle map
A body map on your profile, shaded by how many sets each muscle group got,
with a list beside it and a week-by-week view below. Your profile offers it
once; you can also turn it on in Settings › Features, and choose which
parts to show.

New records, at a glance
The workout summary shows each personal record as a badge, with the number
it beat.

Your choices follow you
Turn a feature on or off once, and your other devices know.

Also
Settings and dialogs fit iPad better.

What to test
- Install Heart on your watch from the Watch app on iPhone, then open it:
  that turns the watch app on. It should show the workout you have running.
- First workout on the watch: allow Health when asked. Heart rate and
  calories should appear within a minute, with no restart.
- Tick, edit and untick sets on the watch; check they land on the phone.
- Switch the phone off (or leave it far away), log sets and Finish on the
  watch, then bring it back. The phone should say it's catching up, show
  how many sets arrived, and open the summary. Health should hold one
  workout, not two.
- Wrist down during a rest: the countdown stays readable (Always On).
- Muscle map: accept or decline the offer on your profile; turn it on in
  Settings › Features and leave parts out. The profile should close up
  around what's left.
- Finish a workout with a heavier set than ever: the record badge appears.
- Sign in on a second device: features you turned on are already on.
