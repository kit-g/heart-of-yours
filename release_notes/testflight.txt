Pause a workout
Stop the clock from the workout's ⋯ menu; ticking a set starts it again.
Left a workout running? Open it from the reminder and Heart offers to end
it at your last set. Pausing works from your Apple Watch too.

Time a set
Timed sets get a stopwatch: tap play in the time cell and it runs, on the
lock screen too. Stop it to log the time.

Keep the screen awake
The screen stays on through your workout, with a sun on the Workout tab
while it does.

Find exercises faster
Search knows other names and abbreviations (OHP, RDL, DB), muscle words,
and forgives typos. The picker opens on what you did last.

Carries, sleds and loaded holds
Two new kinds of exercise: a weight carried a distance, in metres or yards,
and a weight held for a time. Records, charts, History and your Apple Watch
all follow.

Pause, the set stopwatch and keep-awake are off until you turn them on in
Settings › Features.

What to test
- Pause from ⋯: the clock stops; tick a set and it runs again. The
  finished workout's duration leaves out the paused time.
- Leave a workout running, open it from the reminder: it offers "Finished
  at <time>?" and ends at your last set.
- Apple Watch: pause and resume from the watch; the phone follows.
- Set stopwatch: start it from the play button on a timed set, lock the
  phone (the Live Activity shows one clock), pause, resume, Done logs the
  time.
- Keep awake on: the screen never dims mid-workout and the sun shows on
  the Workout tab; it dims normally after finishing.
- Search "ohp", "rdl", "lats", "benchpress", and a typo; the picker opens
  on Recent.
- Log a Farmer's Walk in yards, then metres: the set row, History,
  records, its Charts tab and a goal on its distance all read m or yd,
  never km.
- With the lock-screen workout on, update or reinstall the app mid-workout:
  it comes back on the lock screen on the next launch.
- A workout with planks or push-ups: total volume on its card counts only
  weight lifted.
- Signed out, link a Google account whose email already has a Heart
  account: you land in that account.
