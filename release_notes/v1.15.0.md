# 1.15.0

Both stores. On Android this is also the first release with 1.14's widgets, history moves and Heart imports.

## New

**Programs.** Train to a plan, not just a log. Pick one of 19 ready-made programs, each with the research behind it, or turn a folder of your templates into one. Heart schedules the sessions on the days you choose, fills in each set's target, and adds weight when you earn it: a training max for wave programs, a working weight for the classics, harder steps for bodyweight ones. Starting weights come from your own history, and any lift's can be set by hand. The program page shows the week, when it ends, the goals it implies, and whether one of its sessions is under way. Programs is on by default; Settings › Features turns it off.

**Not today.** Set a program session, or any day, aside with one tap, and say why if you like. The program waits; Undo takes it back.

**Things to go easy on.** List a body area or a movement, such as a knee, heavy weight on the spine or pressing overhead. Heart marks the exercises that are harder on it and suggests kinder swaps. The list stays on your phone.

**Your program in your calendar.** Add a program's sessions to a calendar on your phone, Google's included, with a time or all day.

**Supersets and circuits.** Group exercises in a workout or a template and move through them in turn.

**Rep ranges and targets.** A template's sets can aim at a range, an exact count, a weight or reps in reserve; tapping an empty set fills the target in.

**Sets a week.** On your profile, sets and sessions per week by movement pattern or by muscle, and how push and pull, hinge and squat balance.

**Goals on a pattern or a muscle.** "Presses per week" as well as "bench sets".

**Records on the set.** Each set that set a record carries a badge naming it, on the Done page and in History, and History's card counts them.

**Week starts on.** Choose the day your week starts; every weekly count follows it, and so does your account.

**Last time in this template.** In a workout from a template, Previous can show the last time you ran that template.

## Fixed

- Starting a template over a running workout kept the old workout's name (iOS).
- Android widgets ignored the dark theme's accent.
- The set tick and the workout menu announced nothing to a screen reader.
- The muscle map now counts your own exercises toward their body part, the same way the weekly card and the AI assistant connection count them.
- Translations: plural forms in Russian and French, and a review of the new strings in every language.
