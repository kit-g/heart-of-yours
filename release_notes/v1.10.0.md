# 1.10.0

## Heart on Apple Watch

A watch app for the workout running on your iPhone. The wrist shows the set you're on, with last
time's numbers, and lets you change the weight and reps with − / + or the Digital Crown before
you tick it. Rest counts down on the wrist and taps when it's over; −10s, +10s and Skip work from
there. Swipe for the whole workout to fix or untick a set you already logged. Adding sets or
exercises stays on the phone.

With the phone in your locker, the watch keeps logging. What you do queues and reaches the
iPhone when it's back, with how many sets arrived, and a workout finished on the watch ends when
you finished it. Heart rate and calories are measured by the watch during the workout, shown on
the wrist and saved to Apple Health with it — never sent anywhere else. Opening Heart on the
watch is what turns it on; Settings › Features turns it off. iOS only.

## Muscle map

A body map on your profile, shaded by how many sets each muscle group got over the last 7 or 30
days, with the numbers listed beside it and a week-by-week (or month-by-month) heatmap below. The
profile offers it once; it is also in Settings › Features, where you can choose which of the
three parts to show.

## New records, at a glance

The workout summary shows each personal record as a badge with the value it beat, instead of a
line of text. A first workout on a new account, where every exercise sets a record, gets one
summary badge instead of a wall.

## Your choices follow you

Features you turn on or off — and their options — are saved to your account, so a second device
never asks a question the first one already answered.

## Also

- Settings, the adjust-times dialog and other screens are capped and centred on wide screens.
- The day view of a timeline chart no longer repeats dates on its axis.
- The exercise popup in a workout always shows the library exercise, so About is never missing.
- A new user's Templates page shows the sample templates as soon as they load.
- Opening a workout while another is open no longer stacks pages.
