## New

**Recaps.** Early each month, last month on your profile: workouts, time trained, sets, volume, the muscles and movement patterns you worked, and the records you set. Every month with workouts stays in a Recaps list, and a report can be shared as a card. Worked out on the device from what you logged; never a health value. Optional.

**Copy for AI.** A workout, a stretch of training or one exercise's history as text any AI assistant reads well, through the clipboard or the share sheet. Nothing from Apple Health or Health Connect goes with it. Optional, offered once from a History workout's menu.

**Live records.** A quiet note under a ticked set the moment it beats your best, named the way the Done page names it. Optional, offered the first time a set would have been a record.

**Second look.** Heart points out entries that look like a slip, such as a workout left running for hours, a weight far from what you usually lift, or weight and reps swapped. Keep each one, or leave it out: it stays in your log but stops counting towards records and charts. Your decisions sync. Needs an account. Optional.

**Log a past workout.** For a workout that already happened: say when it was, then what you did, and it goes straight to History. No clock, rest timers or watch. Turn it on in Settings › Features.

**Create an exercise from the picker.** A search with no match offers to create it, while you build a template or during a workout, and a name already in the library says so. Your own exercises can now be tagged with the muscles they work, primary and secondary, on the body map.

**Time a hold from the watch.** With the set stopwatch on, start and stop a timed set from the Apple Watch or a Wear OS watch, and the time lands on the phone.

**Sets a week and the example templates are switches.** Both stay on; Settings › Features turns either off, and the profile, the Workout tab and the shortcuts reflow without them.

## Changed

- An import that brought RPE ratings offers to show them.
- The health copy says "this device" and "your health app", not "your phone", and names every metric Heart reads.

## Fixed

- A new exercise's name refused Cyrillic and other non-Latin scripts.
- A template whose name was only spaces could be saved.
- Finishing a workout could save one with nothing in it; the sheet behind the finish dialog no longer moves.
- Values prescribed by a template counted as typed, so they weren't treated as untouched.
- Apple Watch and Wear OS: on the rest screen, the set's Done no longer covers Skip, and Skip reads in full.
- The empty chart's text on the profile sat off the card's surface.
