# 1.12.0

## New

**Pause a workout.** Stop the clock from the workout's ⋯ menu; ticking a set starts it again, and the paused time stays out of the workout's duration. Left a workout running? Open it from the reminder and Heart offers to end it at your last set. Pausing works from Apple Watch too. Opt-in, in Settings › Features.

**Time a set.** Timed sets get a stopwatch: tap play in the time cell and it runs, on the lock screen too. Stop it to log the time. Opt-in.

**Keep the screen awake.** The screen stays on through a workout, with a sun on the Workout tab while it does. Opt-in.

**Find exercises faster.** Search matches other names and abbreviations (OHP, RDL, DB), muscle words and typos, ranks what you did most recently first, and the picker opens on your recent exercises.

**Carries, sleds and loaded holds.** Two new kinds of exercise: Weighted Distance (a load carried a distance, logged in metres or yards) and Weighted Duration (a load held for a time). Records, charts, goals, History and the Apple Watch follow.

## Fixed

- The lock-screen workout comes back after the app is updated or reinstalled mid-workout.
- A workout card's total volume counts weight lifted only, not seconds of a plank or a count of push-ups.
- Linking a Google account whose email already belongs to a Heart account signs you in to that account instead of failing.

## Under the hood

- The app keeps working when the server or a newer build holds something it doesn't recognise: what it can't read is skipped and left untouched, never deleted, and one unreadable item no longer stops start-up or sync.
- Crash reports from native code are symbolicated, and diagnostic logs reach the team instead of the device console.
