# 1.14.0

iOS first: Android follows once Google Play clears 1.13.

## New

**Heart on your Home Screen.** Three widgets in the widget gallery. Training shows your volume, workouts, time, sets or reps over the last week or months, or an Apple Health reading instead, and fits the Lock Screen too. Start starts a workout or one of your templates in one tap. Goals shows how far along your goals are.

**Start from the icon.** With Shortcuts on, touching and holding the app icon offers a blank workout and your templates.

**Move an exercise's history.** Move history to… on an exercise's menu puts everything logged under it onto another exercise: a wrong import guess, or a custom exercise made before finding the library one, no longer stays that way. A preview counts what moves and says what doesn't. Needs an account.

**Bring your Heart history back.** Import reads Heart's own exports. The JSON file restores workouts, templates and folders, custom exercises and goals; the CSV brings workouts and their sets.

## Fixed

**A weight of 0 sticks.** A set's weight set to 0 during a workout, typed or filled from Previous, was never stored: the set kept its old weight, the watch and the lock screen showed it, and Done on the watch logged it. (#383)

**Sign in with Apple** is one sheet and signs you in directly.
