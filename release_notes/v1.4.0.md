## Your workouts in Apple Health and Health Connect

Finished workouts now save to Apple Health and Health Connect, so your rings credit your lifting.
Heart writes how long you trained and what kind of session it was — a swim logs as a swim, a lift
as strength training — and nothing else.

It never estimates calories. Without a watch there is no measurement, only a guess, and a made-up
number sitting next to your watch's own reading is worse than no number at all.

If you have already connected your health data, Heart asks once, right after you finish a workout.
There's a "Save workouts to Health" row in Heart's settings to turn it on or off later.
