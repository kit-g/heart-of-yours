# 1.13.0

## New

**Heart, by voice.** Ask Siri to start a workout or one of your templates, finish it, start, extend or skip your rest, or log the set you're on. Ask it things too: your record on an exercise, when you last did it, how many workouts this week. Everything is in the Shortcuts app as well. On Android, the app icon's shortcuts start a workout, blank or from a template. Opt-in, in Settings › Features.

**Rest and Done on the lock screen.** The Live Activity and the Android workout notification get buttons: start, extend or skip your rest, and Done ticks off the set up next without opening the app. A rest now survives the app being closed.

**Duplicate a template.** Duplicate on a template's ⋯ menu makes a copy in the same folder, examples included; a second copy is "(copy 2)".

**Bring your Hevy history.** Import takes Hevy's CSV export as well as Strong's. A file that can't be read automatically is kept and imported by hand, and you're told when it's in.

**One account, every sign-in.** Connect Apple, Google and a password to the same account in Settings › Account, and disconnect the ones you don't use. An email that already has an account under another sign-in now says so instead of failing silently.

**Developer API.** Create, see and revoke read-only personal access tokens in Settings › Developer API, and read your own training log from a script, a spreadsheet or an AI assistant. Health data is never part of it.

## Fixed

- A Strong import asks which unit the export is in; a file exported in lb no longer comes in as kg.
- "Previous" shows the last finished session, never the workout in progress, including after a restart.
- Editing a past workout saves it as it stands, and its Previous column is the session before it.
- An existing Apple account signs in with a fresh token instead of failing with a duplicate credential, and the name Apple gives at sign-in stays on the account.
- Deleting an account leaves nothing of it on screen afterwards.
- An account without a name shows its email instead of "?".
- The splash stays up until the app is ready, and no longer waits on the network.
- A rest skipped on the watch or lock screen closes the in-app countdown; a rest started from outside the app shows it.
- The last set's rest alert names its exercise.
- A value typed on the phone reaches the watch and the lock screen.
- Corrected copy: the finish warning (only ticked sets are saved), "1 day" in account deletion, and a shortcut to a deleted template now says so.

## Under the hood

- The exercise library is read one entry at a time: a category this build doesn't know drops that exercise, not the whole catalogue. The App Store's 1.9.0 lost its library this way to the new Weighted Duration category.
- Workouts, templates, goals and folders that a newer build filled are kept untouched when this build edits them.
