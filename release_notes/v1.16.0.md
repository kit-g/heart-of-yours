# 1.16.0

Both stores, and the first release of the Wear OS app.

## New

**Heart on Wear OS.** Log sets from a Wear OS watch, with rest, last time's numbers and your heart rate, which goes into Health Connect if you let it. Ambient mode, an Ongoing Activity, a Tile and a complication; leave the phone in the locker and the watch keeps logging, then hands the sets over.

**Plate calculator.** While you type a barbell or machine weight, a strip over the keyboard says which plates go on each side, each plate drawn with its weight on it. Set the bar for each exercise (Olympic, women's, EZ, trap, Smith, none or your own weight), untick the plates your gym doesn't have, and a weight the plates can't make offers the nearest one either side. Optional.

**Warm-up sets.** Worked out from your first working set and rounded to what you can load, from the exercise's menu or the one offer under it. The ramp is yours to change in Settings › Features. Optional.

**Default rest.** A rest after every set of an exercise with no timer of its own: 2:30 for compound lifts, 1:30 for isolation, 1:00 for bodyweight and cardio, each editable or set to no rest. A program's planned rest and an exercise's own timer still win. Optional.

**Add a session to a program.** A template can join a program you're already running, from the program's page or from its menu in the program's folder, and leave it again. Your weeks, cycle and starting weights stay as they are; the new session counts from the day it joined, and its lifts take starting weights from your history.

**A muscle map for templates.** See which muscles a template works while you plan it and in the start dialog. Part of the optional muscle map.

**Training charts.** Volume, working sets and average workout length by week or month, and a heatmap of the days you trained a lift, for your profile.

**Apple Watch.** A set fits on one screen on every watch size, Done included, and its values are compact and centred.

**Clearer Spanish, French and Russian** across the app, its permission prompts and these notes.

## Changed

- An exercise's menu in a workout is the app's own popup at a fixed width: rows in a new order, long superset names wrap instead of pushing it off screen, and Remove sits apart at the end.
- A ready-made program's page shows its facts as badges, its session cards use their width, and its rows match.

## Fixed

- A bodyweight exercise done without added weight, showed no Previous the next time; on the Apple Watch, no last time either.
- The exercise picker's Target and Category buttons sat off-centre.
- A program opened from After this kept the previous program's scroll position, and its row stayed highlighted on iPad.
- Apple Watch: a set planned in a template or program started at zero instead of its target; weighted holds and carries asked for a count.
