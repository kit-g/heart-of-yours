## Bring your Strong history

Lifted with Strong before? Import your whole history: Settings → Your data → Import workout
history. Pick the CSV file from Strong's export email and Heart brings over your workouts, sets
and exercises. Before anything is written you see what the file holds — and exercises that don't
match Heart's library are yours to approve one by one, so junk names stay behind. Importing the
same file twice is safe: anything already here is skipped, never duplicated.

## Smaller things

- Settings are organized into sections — appearance, units, health, your data, account and app
  each have their own place, and the section headers announce themselves to VoiceOver and
  TalkBack.
