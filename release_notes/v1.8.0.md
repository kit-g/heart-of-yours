## Exercise notes

Add a note to any exercise while you train — a cue, a setup, a weight to try next time. Sign in
and you can pin a note to an exercise, so it comes back on every workout that includes it,
including workouts you start from a template.

## A calendar view of your history

History has a calendar button. Open it to see your training month by month, scroll back through
older months, and tap any day to open that workout.

## A new look

New logotype, new app icon, and a launch screen that follows light and dark. Fresh installs open
in Ink; the other themes are where they were, in Settings.

## Apple Health prompts in your language

The Apple Health permission prompts now read in Spanish, French and Russian, not just English.

## Fixes

Bodyweight exercises got two bad ones: the average working weight chart broke outright, and sets
logged at bodyweight showed no reps in exercise history. Both are fixed, along with a batch of
smaller things.
