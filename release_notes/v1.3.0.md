## Health data on your profile

Heart can now read resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, steps, active energy and
body mass from Apple Health and Health Connect, and chart them on your profile. It stays on the
device — nothing is uploaded — and you can delete Heart's copy whenever you like. When you grant
access, allow past data too, or the charts only reach back thirty days.

## Charts you can scroll

Exercise and health charts now go back through your whole history instead of a few weeks. Switch
between 1M, 3M, 1Y and All, drag to move through time, and tap any health card for the full chart
with axes.

## VoiceOver and TalkBack

Buttons, charts, timers and inputs across the app now announce themselves.

## Smaller things

- Deleting a workout from your history asks first.
- The rest timer follows the exercise you're actually resting on.
- The Previous column no longer shows an empty pill.
- Opening a Heart link from outside the app lands on the right page, even from a cold start.
