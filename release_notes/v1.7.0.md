## Heart works without an account

Open the app and start training. No sign-up, no email, no waiting: your workouts, templates,
folders, custom exercises and goals live on the phone from the first session, and so does any
health data you let Heart read — that has always stayed on the device and still does. A short
intro on first launch explains the trade in three screens, and you can skip it at any point.

Everything is there. Log a workout, build a template, set a goal, read your charts. The only
things absent are the ones that genuinely need an account, and they are absent rather than
dangled behind a prompt.

## Log in whenever you like, and bring your history with you

Sign in from an account-free session and everything you logged first goes up to the account —
workouts with every set, templates and their folders, your own exercises, unit settings and
goals. Your profile shows it going, counts what landed, and says so when it is done. Stop and
start the app mid-way and it picks up where it left off rather than sending anything twice.

If you sign in to an account you already had on another phone, the two are merged rather than
one overwriting the other.

## Your whole history, not just the recent part

Until now the app held whatever it had fetched, which for a long history meant the most recent
part of it — and the weekly chart, your goals and your records were all counted from that. They
are now counted from everything. On the first launch after signing in, the rest of your history
downloads in the background, with a line on your profile while it does, and the numbers fill in
behind it.

## Take your data with you — or erase it

**Export my data**, in Settings, writes everything on the phone to a file that is yours to keep:
JSON for the whole picture, or CSV with one row per set for a spreadsheet. It reads only what is
on the device, so it works offline and with or without an account. Health data is never in it.

**Erase my data**, also in Settings, is the other half: without an account there is nothing on a
server to delete, so this wipes the phone — workouts, templates, exercises, goals, chart settings
and Heart's copy of your health readings. Your phone's own health records are untouched.

## Smaller things

- Import from Strong now brings the whole file down to the phone, not only its most recent part.
- Workouts no longer lose their exercises after syncing, and any that had been left bare are
  repaired.
- An abandoned session from months ago can no longer come back looking like a workout in
  progress.
- Dialogs follow a switch to dark mode while they are open.
- The weekly chart stays readable when a week holds hundreds of workouts.
